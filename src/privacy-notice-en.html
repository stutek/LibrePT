Client Privacy Notice

For the client to read. Given by the trainer, not by LibrePT.

This is the document linked from the consent email and SMS the app sends. Consent is only valid if the person giving it was informed first (GDPR Art. 7(2) and Art. 13), and this notice is that information — the consent form is the record that it was given.

Trainer: fill in the bracketed fields before sharing this. The bracketed items are the only parts that differ between trainers; everything else describes how LibrePT stores data and is already accurate.

Who is responsible for your data

[Trainer / business name], contact [email], [phone], is the data controller for the training records described below. That means the trainer — not the makers of the LibrePT app — decides what is recorded and answers your requests about it.

LibrePT is free, open-source software your trainer runs on their own device. Its makers operate no server, receive no copy of your data, and cannot see, retrieve, or delete it.

What is recorded about you

  • Identity and contact: your name, and any email address or phone number you give.
  • Training data: your goals, session dates, exercises, sets, reps, loads, and how each session went.
  • Health information: injuries, pain, mobility limitations, and similar notes needed to train you safely. Under GDPR Art. 9 this is special category data, which is why your explicit consent is asked for rather than assumed.

Nothing is recorded automatically about your device or browsing — the app has no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no advertising.

Why, and on what legal basis

To plan your sessions, adapt your programme over time, and train you safely. The legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)), given on the consent form. You can withdraw it at any time — see below.

Where it is stored, and who else can see it

  • Primarily on your trainer's own device, inside the browser's local storage. There is no LibrePT server.
  • Optionally, a backup copy in your trainer's own Google Drive, in a private app-only area that is not visible in their normal Drive file list and not accessible to other apps. Google acts as the storage provider here, under your trainer's own Google account; the data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google, though not in a form Google itself cannot read.
  • Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or handed to third parties.
  • If your trainer uses an AI assistant to help plan training, records are anonymized first — names and contact details stripped — using the app's built-in anonymized-copy tool.

How long it is kept

For as long as you are a client, plus [retention period, e.g. 2 years] after your last session, so your training history is still available if you return. After that it is deleted. The signed consent form itself is kept by your trainer for as long as they hold your records, as proof that consent was given.

Your rights

You can, at any time and free of charge, ask your trainer to:

  • See what is held about you, and get a copy in a portable file (Art. 15, Art. 20) — the app exports your full history on request.
  • Correct anything inaccurate (Art. 16).
  • Delete your records — the "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17). Deleting your profile removes it from your trainer's device and from the next cloud backup.
  • Withdraw your consent (Art. 7(3)). Withdrawal is as easy as giving it: tell your trainer, in any form. It stops future processing and does not make what happened before unlawful.
  • Complain to your national data protection authority if you believe your data has been mishandled. In Slovenia this is the Information Commissioner (ip-rs.si); every EU country has an equivalent, listed by the EDPB.

Requests go to your trainer at the contact details above.
